# Security & Software Engineering Research Lab (S<sup>2</sup>E Lab)

[**S**ecurity & **S**oftware **E**ngineering Research Lab (S<sup>2</sup>E Lab)](https://s2e-lab.github.io/) is a research lab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering of the University of Notre Dame led by Dr. [Joanna C. S. Santos](https://joannacss.github.io). The lab is dedicated to studying the broad area of software engineering, primarily focusing on software security.
